EMBER PACT

Three duels. Four elements. One pact to keep.

How a duel works

  • Play cards by clicking them. Each card costs embers — you gain 3 embers every turn.
  • Resonance: play two cards of the same element in one turn and the second hits +2 harder.
  • Trine: play three different elements in one turn to draw a card.
  • Read the enemy's intent above the table, then answer it: guard against a strike, strike through a guard.
  • Reduce the enemy to 0 life to win the duel. Fall to 0 and the pact breaks.

An original game. No network, no account, just cards.
